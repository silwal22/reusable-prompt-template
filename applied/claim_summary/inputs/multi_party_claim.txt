Claim ID 2451028. Insured reports a three-vehicle collision on a highway during heavy rain. Driver states they were in the middle lane and a vehicle cut in front of them, causing a chain reaction. The other driver alleges the insured braked abruptly without signaling. Police report lists two third-party vehicles, one minor injury, and no airbag deployment. Damage includes front bumper, left headlamp, and rear quarter panel on the insured vehicle; no tow or mechanical failure recorded. The insured has no independent witness; the opposing driver has a passenger witness who reported the insured was speeding.
